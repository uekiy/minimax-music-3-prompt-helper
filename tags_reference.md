# MiniMax Music 3 Prompt Helper - Tag Reference

English | [日本語](tags_reference.ja.md)

This document explains the Genre, Mood, and Vocal suggestion pills included in the helper app. Use them as vocabulary ideas when building a prompt.

> These are general descriptions for the helper's input suggestions, not an official MiniMax Music 3 tag specification.

## Genre / Style

### Pop and dance

- **J-Pop**: Japanese pop with catchy melodies and often detailed chord progressions.
- **K-Pop**: Polished dance production, strong low end, and prominent synthesizers.
- **Acoustic Pop**: Warm pop centered on acoustic guitar, piano, and organic textures.
- **EDM**: Electronic dance music designed around energetic club or festival rhythms.
- **Future Bass**: Elastic rhythms, bright chords, and wide, modulated synth bass.

### Electronic and alternative

- **Cyberpunk**: Futuristic, dystopian electronics with heavy synth bass and industrial textures.
- **Synthwave**: A retro-futuristic interpretation of 1980s synth-pop and soundtrack sounds.
- **Lo-Fi Hip Hop**: Relaxed hip-hop with deliberately worn textures such as vinyl noise.
- **Chiptune**: Retro game-inspired music built from 8-bit-style electronic tones.
- **Vaporwave**: Nostalgic, surreal music inspired by processed 1980s and 1990s commercial sounds.

### Rock and band music

- **Rock**: Energetic music led by electric guitar, bass, and drums.
- **Metal**: Heavier and more aggressive rock with distorted riffs and forceful performances.

### Jazz and world music

- **Jazz**: Swinging rhythms, rich harmony, and improvisation.
- **Bossa Nova**: Light Brazilian music combining samba rhythm with jazz harmony.
- **Celtic**: Pastoral or mystical music featuring instruments such as pipes and tin whistle.
- **Folk**: Tradition-rooted songs and instrumentals centered on acoustic instruments.

### Classical and orchestral

- **Classical**: Formal music using piano, strings, and other orchestral instruments.
- **Orchestral Epic**: Large, dramatic full-orchestra music suited to cinematic climaxes.

### R&B, soul, and groove

- **R&B**: Polished grooves paired with expressive vocals.
- **Soul**: Passionate vocal music with gospel and blues roots.
- **Funk**: Syncopated rhythms, cutting guitar, and prominent bass grooves.
- **Reggae**: Jamaican-rooted music with offbeat accents and a relaxed pulse.

### Video and background music

- **Corporate**: Clean, dependable background music for presentations and company videos.
- **Cinematic**: Wide, dramatic production for films and cinematic video.
- **Ambient**: Atmospheric music focused on space and texture rather than a strong beat.
- **Vlog / Lifestyle**: Light, approachable music for everyday and lifestyle videos.
- **Documentary**: Restrained, thoughtful music for factual or explanatory content.
- **Trailer Music**: High-impact, dramatic music shaped like a film trailer cue.

## Mood / Emotion

### Positive and active

- **Energetic**: Active, powerful, and full of momentum.
- **Uplifting**: Emotionally elevating and exciting.
- **Happy**: Bright, cheerful, and joyful.
- **Inspiring**: Forward-looking and motivating.
- **Upbeat**: Positive, lively, and rhythmically buoyant.
- **Hopeful**: Optimistic and suggestive of a brighter future.

### Relaxed and gentle

- **Relaxing**: Calm and unhurried, with a soothing effect.
- **Chill**: Comfortable, laid-back, and easygoing.
- **Dreamy**: Soft, floating, and fantasy-like.
- **Neutral**: Balanced and emotionally restrained.

### Emotional

- **Romantic**: Sweet, intimate, and suggestive of love.
- **Sentimental**: Emotionally reflective and moving.
- **Nostalgic**: Evocative of memories and the past.
- **Melancholy**: Beautifully subdued, pensive, and wistful.
- **Sad**: Focused on sorrow, loss, or grief.

### Tense, dark, and powerful

- **Epic**: Grand, heroic, and large in scale.
- **Aggressive**: Intense, forceful, and confrontational.
- **Dramatic**: Theatrical, dynamic, and strongly shaped.
- **Suspenseful**: Tense, uncertain, and mysterious.
- **Dark**: Heavy, ominous, or unsettling.
- **Mysterious**: Enigmatic, secretive, and unfamiliar.
- **Quirky**: Unusual, playful, and slightly comic.

## Vocal Details

### Basic setup

- **Female vocal**: A female lead or vocal character.
- **Male vocal**: A male lead or vocal character.
- **Instrumental**: No vocals; instruments only.

### Tone and delivery

- **Powerful**: Strong, projected vocal delivery.
- **Breathy**: Soft vocal tone with audible breath and intimacy.
- **Clear**: Clean, transparent, and direct vocal tone.
- **Raspy**: Rough, husky, or grainy vocal tone.
- **Whispering**: Very quiet, close, whispered delivery.
- **Deep voice**: A low, full vocal register.
- **High pitch**: A high vocal register or frequent high notes.

### Effects and multiple singers

- **Choir**: A group of singers performing together.
- **Duet**: Two lead vocalists sharing the performance.
- **Vocoder**: A synthetic, processed, robot-like vocal effect.

### Voice elements for background music

- **Wordless**: Non-lexical vocals used like an instrument.
- **Humming**: Gentle humming or closed-mouth vocalization.
- **Backing Vocals**: Supporting harmonies or background vocal layers.
